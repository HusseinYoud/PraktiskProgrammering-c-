B. Hydrogen ground state by shooting
========================================

Boundary condition near r = 0
----------------------------------------
Assume
    f(r) = a*r + b*r^2 + O(r^3).
Then
    f''(r) = 2*b + O(r).
Inserting into
    -f''/2 - f/r = E*f
and comparing the constant terms gives
    -b - a = 0,
so b = -a.
The normalization is arbitrary, so choosing a = 1 gives
    f(r) = r - r^2 + O(r^3).

C. Optimized quadratic line search
========================================

The Newton routine used in parts A and B contains:
1. One Jacobian matrix allocated before the iteration
   loop and updated in place at every Newton step.
2. A quadratic interpolation line search based on
       phi(alpha) = ||f(x + alpha*Dx)||^2 / 2.
3. Backtracking until the sufficient-decrease
   condition is satisfied or alpha reaches alpha_min.

The successful Rosenbrock, Himmelblau, and hydrogen
calculations above test this optimized implementation.
